# Visual credits

Interface images and the video demonstration are captures of the working RailVerify prototype. The sample bill and dashboard records are explicitly fictional. Mandatory introduction and sponsor closing artwork comes from the organizer-supplied SYI26 template. Manrope and Space Grotesk use the SIL Open Font License, included with the font archive and the web fonts.

The train-meal image is a conceptual illustration generated using the built-in image tool. It is not a photograph of a reported event.

## Original generation prompt

Create a photorealistic editorial illustration for a student railway consumer protection pitch, landscape 16:9. Interior of an Indian passenger train, blue upholstered seat and window with soft afternoon countryside outside. Close foreground on the right half a disposable foil meal tray with rice, dal, roti and vegetables on a fold-down table, beside a clear sealed 1 litre water bottle with a plain blank pale blue label, no brand marks. A passenger's hand holds an ordinary paper receipt beside the meal, receipt has no legible text. Warm real Indian travel feeling, detailed realistic food, premium documentary photography, shallow depth of field. Composition puts main objects in right two-thirds and leaves left third dark navy and softly defocused for headline text. No overlaid typography, no logos, no watermark, no dramatic danger or confrontation. This is a conceptual illustrative scene, not a real reported event.

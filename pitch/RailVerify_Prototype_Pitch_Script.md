## 00:21–00:35 · Screen 3 · An uncomfortable decision

For someone travelling alone, a small overcharge can become an uncomfortable decision. RailVerify gives passengers a private price check, in a familiar conversation, before they decide whether to report.

Delivery · Keep the example human and conversational. This is an illustrative situation.

## 00:35–00:48 · Screen 4 · A documented need

Railways serves about fifty-eight crore meals each year. Following catering complaint inquiries, fines reached two-point-six crore rupees over three years. Fair pricing matters at this scale.

Delivery · State the scale clearly. Do not call these numbers proof of under-reporting.

## 00:48–01:25 · Screen 5 · The working prototype

Here is our working web prototype. Upload a bill, and OCR reads the text. Confirm the meal, service and amount. In this sample, one-fifty paid, eighty listed. Seventy rupees difference, with the official source beside it. Water checks use the current bottle size and price. Review the details, approve a local report, and download the evidence. Then continue through official RailMadad. WhatsApp is our planned passenger channel, using this same checking logic.

Delivery · Let the recorded prototype do the visual work. Emphasize the three numbers. The bill is a clearly labeled fictional sample.

## 01:25–01:39 · Screen 6 · What testing taught us

Our end-to-end test read a sample bill, checked its price and downloaded the report. Offline drafts synchronized when the connection returned. Testing also reinforced a rule. Passengers must confirm the evidence.

Delivery · These are software checks, not passenger validation. Briefly pause before the last sentence.

## 01:39–02:05 · Screen 7 · The next proof

Next, we will test with thirty passengers. In three months, we aim for a railway-approved pilot, measuring accuracy and reporting time. In twelve months, we want to turn verified reports into useful train-and-vendor patterns for catering teams, through a paid service. Passengers stay free. The dashboard here uses illustrative data. RailVerify helps every passenger make an informed choice, without starting an argument.

## YouTube description

A ₹150 bill. An ₹80 listed meal. What would you do? RailVerify is a working web prototype that reads a bill, checks confirmed purchase details against railway tariffs, and prepares a report for passenger review. This pitch demonstrates bill OCR, tariff comparisons, evidence downloads and offline report synchronization. WhatsApp is the planned passenger channel. Official grievances follow RailMadad’s separate identity and submission process. The sample bill and dashboard records shown are illustrative.
